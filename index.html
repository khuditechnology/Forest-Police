PRIVACY POLICY - Forest Police: Hunter Chase

Khudi Technology - last updated 27 October 2026

Who We Are

Khudi Technology ("we") publishes Forest Police: Hunter Chase (the "game") for Android. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information on your device.

What The Game Stores

The game keeps a small settings file on your phone: your control choices (steering, gearbox, graphics, sound, reminders), the vehicle and paint you picked, your mission progress and best fuel figures, and whether the Google Play rating card and the reminders question have already been shown. Nothing in that file identifies you. It never leaves the phone, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

What The Game Does Not Do

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, e-mail or phone number is asked for or stored.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are read.
  • No crash-reporting service is built in. Anonymous play statistics are described under ANALYTICS.
  • Nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

The game asks for VIBRATE, so the phone can rumble when the truck is hit. For the ads, the play statistics and the update check it also needs INTERNET and network state, and Google's ad library adds the advertising ID permission. On Android 13 and newer it may ask to show notifications, for the reminders described below; you can say no. So that reminders already set survive a restart of the phone, the game may also run for a moment when the phone starts up (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED). The game itself plays offline; only the ads need a connection.

Reminders

If you stop playing for a while, the game can show up to three short reminders on your phone - about a day, three days and a week after you last played - and then no more until you play again. The game itself sets them up on your phone: no server sends them and no push token or other identifier is created for them. Whether a reminder was shown or opened, your answer to the reminders question and whether reminders are switched on are part of the anonymous play statistics described under ANALYTICS; nothing else about them leaves the phone. On Android 13 and newer they appear only if you allow notifications when asked. You can switch them off at any time in Options -> REMINDERS or in your phone's notification settings.

Analytics

To learn how the game is played and where it needs improving, the game sends anonymous play statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: which missions and free drives are started and how they end, how long they take, which menu screens and features (refuel, repair, radar, the garage) are used, when and where the game is left (in a mission or on a menu) and what the Back button did, whether a rewarded ad was watched, whether a reminder was shown or opened, the answer to the reminders question and whether reminders are switched on, the vehicle and graphics setting chosen, and the app version. With them Google records a random installation ID, the device model and operating system, the country (worked out from the IP address, which is not kept) and, for ad measurement, the advertising ID. No name, e-mail, exact location or anything you type is collected - the game has no such fields. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the data is kept for up to 14 months. You can reset the advertising ID in your phone's Google settings at any time, and uninstalling the game ends the collection.

Update Check

When the game starts it may fetch a small text file from our website (khuditechnology.github.io) that says which version is on Google Play, so it can offer you an update. That request carries nothing about you.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: video ads you choose to watch for a reward (a refuel or a repair from mission 2, a second chance after running out of fuel or wrecking the truck, more rope or rounds, the radar, another weather, or a locked truck for one mission), and now and then a full-screen ad between missions - never while you are driving. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse location derived from it, and information about your device and the ad, under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and ad terms. In the European Economic Area and the UK the game first asks for your consent with Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in Options -> PRIVACY CHOICES. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings. We do not receive any of that data ourselves.

Ratings

After your first win the game may show Google Play's own rating card. A rating or review you leave there goes to Google Play under Google's terms; the game and we receive nothing from it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The game contains cartoon violence (shooting at and arresting poachers). If you believe a child has used it to contact us, write to the address below and we will delete anything we hold - which, today, is nothing.

Your Rights

We hold no personal data about you: the statistics above are anonymous and reach us only as totals, so there is nothing we could match to a request to access, correct or delete; the settings file is yours to delete with the app. Questions about this policy, or about data handled by Google when ads are shown or statistics are reported, can be sent to khuditechnology@gmail.com.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting anything new, this policy will be updated in the app and at the hosted address before that version ships, with the date above changed.
